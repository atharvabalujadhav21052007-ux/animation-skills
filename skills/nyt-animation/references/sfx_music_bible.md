# NOT YOUR TYPE — Sound Design & Meme SFX Bible

Sound design in NYT is not background ambiance; it is the **rhythmic punctuation of the jokes**.

---

## 1. The Pre-Punchline Silence Rule (Crucial)

**Never allow background music to play continuously through a punchline.**
- When moving from an escalated anime scream or chaotic debate to the final flat punchline:
  - **Cut the music abruptly 0.4 to 0.6 seconds before the punchline begins.**
  - Let dead silence or a faint awkward cricket / record scratch sound fill the gap.
  - The sudden absence of sound creates the comedic tension that makes the flat reaction funny.

---

## 2. Core SFX Library & Trigger Guidelines

| SFX Name | Audio Characteristic | When to Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| **`vine_boom`** | 40Hz sub-bass punch with distortion | Eye-contact shock, absurd realization, price reveal. |
| **`anime_whoosh`** | High-speed resonant air whip | Camera jump-cuts, rapid arm snaps, sudden scene transitions. |
| **`metal_pipe`** | Resonant harmonic metallic clatter | Physical impact, dropped utensil, utter failure. |
| **`cartoon_bonk`** | Descending pitch rubber mallet pop | Small awkward mistake or goofy realization. |
| **`record_scratch`** | Quick vinyl disc screech & halt | Abrupt interruption, reality check, music cutoff. |
| **`bgm_quirky_loop`**| 120 BPM bouncy pizzicato walking bass | Casual banter, storytelling, setups. |

---

## 3. Mix Levels & Ducking

- **Dialogue:** `+1.0 dB` (Always crystal clear above everything).
- **SFX Hits:** `+0.5 dB` to `+1.5 dB` (Loud, sharp, instantaneous impact).
- **Background Music:** `-14 dB` to `-18 dB` (Subtle groove under dialogue, completely muted on punchlines).
