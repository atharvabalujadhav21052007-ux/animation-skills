## Visual Language & Rules
1. **Palette**:
   - Background: Warm Notion Paper `#f7f6f3` (Light) or Slate `#191919` (Dark).
   - Text: Signature graphite `#37352f` (Light) or `#e6e6e6` (Dark).
   - Border: Hairline `rgba(55, 53, 47, 0.09)`.
   - Accent Tags: Pastel Yellow (`#fef9c3`), Blue (`#dbeafe`), Green (`#dcfce7`), Purple (`#f3e8ff`), Coral (`#fee2e2`).
   - Highlighter: Warm marker yellow underlay `#fde047`.
2. **Components**:
   - **Notion Avatar**: Minimalist black-and-white character with expressive eyes, hair, blinking, speaking mouth, and props (coffee mug, thinking pose, shocked pose).
   - **UI Cards**: Callout blocks, toggle lists, database rows, checklists, progress bars.
   - **Interactive Cursor**: Vector mouse pointer moving organically across cards, clicking with circular ripple waves.
   - **Dynamic Typography**: Clean serif (Lora) and sans-serif (Poppins/Inter) with typewriter typing and keyword highlights.
3. **Audio & Pacing**:
   - High-energy voiceover generated via Gemini API TTS.
   - Synced word-by-word timestamps.
   - Leveled to -14 LUFS broadcast standard.
